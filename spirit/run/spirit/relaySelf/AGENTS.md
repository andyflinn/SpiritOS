# The relay's own verbs

A relay answers these to its members and its owner, as posts addressed to its own key. A member may call card, face, removePeer, rename and search; the owner may call every verb. The body `api` answers the verbs the asker may call, each with its description from its file here (goal/G30.5); the owner also reads this folder through its FS verbs (the AGENTS verbs went with goal/G30.4).
